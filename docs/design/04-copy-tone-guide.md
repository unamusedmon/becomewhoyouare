# 04 — Copy & Tone Guide

> The app is **a stern-but-loving friend who has read Nietzsche and also knows how your brain works.** It never nags, never moralizes, never fakes enthusiasm. It's calm, a little dry, occasionally funny, and always on your side.

## The division of labor

| Layer | Its job | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **Science** | Mechanics: defaults, timing, buffers, nudges, sorting, first steps | Buttons, timers, notifications, the Now card. Plain, short, concrete. |
| **Philosophy** | Meaning: identity, resilience after a slip, what deserves a place in your life | Onboarding, the recurrence question, slip insights, overcoming evidence, at most one aphorism per screen. |

**Rule zero: the philosophy never overrides the science.** If a sentence sounds profound but adds load, guilt or "willpower", cut it.

## Voice, in five traits

1. **Direct.** Say the thing. "Open Gmail and type the subject line." Not "Why not try getting started on that email?"
2. **Warm without being sweet.** No exclamation marks, no "You've got this!!", no emoji confetti in text. Warmth shows in *what* the app notices, not in adjectives.
3. **Dry humor, light feet.** Nietzsche: *"What is good is light; whatever is divine moves on tender feet"* (*The Case of Wagner* §1). Play is allowed and welcome. Sarcasm aimed at the user never is.
4. **Non-moral.** Nothing is good or bad, owed or overdue. Things are chosen, started, resting, released, or not yet. Nietzsche traces the German *Schuld* (guilt) back to *Schulden* (debts) in *Genealogy of Morals* II; the app refuses that bookkeeping. **Productivity is not a debt.**
5. **Honest.** Never invent praise. Overcoming evidence is only shown when the data supports it. If a deadline really passed, say so plainly and move to options.

## Words

### Never use

| Banned | Why | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| overdue, late, behind, missed, failed | Debt and shame framing | "still here", "slipped", "past its date" (hard deadlines only) |
| should, must, need to, have to | Moral imperative (the camel's "thou shalt") | "you could", "next:", or just the action itself |
| lazy, procrastinating, unproductive | Character judgment | Describe the task's structure, not the person |
| discipline, willpower, push through, grind, hustle, crush it | Willpower models of self-control have weak support (the large ego-depletion replications, Hagger et al. 2016 and Vohs et al. 2021 with 36 labs, both found effects near zero) and they shame ADHD brains | "make it easier", "shrink it", "set it up so it starts itself" |
| despite your ADHD, even with ADHD, fix, overcome your ADHD | Frames the brain as defective | "your brain works differently", "for how you work" |
| streak broken, you lost your streak | Loss punishment | Streaks don't exist (see Progress) |
| productivity, optimize, efficiency, KPIs | Corporate task manager voice | "force", "what you're building", "today's allocation" |
| proven, clinically shown, science-backed, "for ADHD" as a claim of effect, dopamine hit/boost | Almost every mechanism here is tested in people in general, not in adults with ADHD, and brain-chemistry talk is pop neuroscience (see 06) | Say what it does: "plans tied to a moment get done more often", "you'll see if it helps you" |
| just (as in "just do it") | Minimizes real difficulty | Drop the word |
| "What doesn't kill me makes me stronger" | Romanticizes suffering. Real quote (*Twilight*, Maxims §8), wrong lesson for this app | — |

### Prefer

- **force / energy** over time or capacity ("You have about two episodes of deep force today")
- **build / make / impose** over complete or finish, for meaningful work
- **start** as a celebrated verb in its own right
- **rest / release** for stopping work, never "abandon"
- **becoming** for the long arc; **today** for the immediate. Never "goals".

## Formatting rules

- **The first step is the headline.** Task title is secondary text above it.
- Sentence case everywhere. No ALL CAPS except the task title on the recurrence card.
- **Max 2 sentences** on any card. **Max ~60 characters** in a notification title, ~110 in the body.
- **Durations always in lived units first**, clock time second and dimmer: "about one laundry cycle · 45 min".
- **No red, ever, for anything time-related.** Red is reserved for destructive actions (delete forever). Slipped or past-date states use the muted color with an amber accent at most.
- **Aphorisms:** at most one per screen, at most three per day, always skippable with a tap, never on the Now card, never in a notification, never during focus mode. Default setting is "rare".

## Copy by moment

### Onboarding (the three metamorphoses)

Onboarding loosely follows Zarathustra's *"On the Three Metamorphoses"*: the **camel** carries "thou shalt", the **lion** says "I will", the **child** says a sacred "yes". Don't explain this. Let it shape the order.

1. **Camel (unload):** "Dump everything you're carrying. Talk or type. Don't sort it." *(voice capture, big mic button)*
2. **Lion (choose):** "Most of that was handed to you. Which of it do you actually want?" *(quick swipe: keep / not mine / not now)*
3. **Child (affirm):** "Who are you becoming? Not a goal. A person." *(free text, examples: "someone who writes every week", "a dad who's actually in the room")*
4. **Amor fati:** "Your brain works differently. It has real costs, and real powers: hyperfocus, speed under pressure, novelty-hunting, sideways ideas. This app is built for the brain you have."
5. **Opt-in, once:** "Want an occasional question about whether things deserve their place in your life? You can turn it off anytime." [Yes] [Not now]

### The Now card

- Header: `Next` (never "Due", never "Today's tasks")
- Body: the first step, large. `Open Gmail and type the subject line`
- Sub: `Email to landlord · about one song · fits your energy`
- Buttons: **`I did it`** · `still too big` · `not now`

### Completion

Text is minimal; the visual does the work (vivid animation, haptic, the progress art grows a little).

- First step done: **"Started."** Then: `Keep going` · `That counts. Stop here.`
- Task done: **"Done."** Optionally one of a rotating set: "Imposed on the world." · "That exists now because of you." · "One less weight."
- Never: "Great job!", "Amazing!", "You're on fire!"

### Slips (failure as data)

Silent on the first and second slip. The task just stays, unchanged in color. On the third:

> **This one keeps slipping.** That usually means it's built wrong, not that you are. Shrink it, break it up, or let it go?
> `shrink` · `break it up` · `let it go` · `it's fine`

Reflection prompt (shown once per insight, optional answer field):

> "What does this avoidance tell you about how the task is actually built?"

Variants to rotate:
- "Something in this task is heavier than it looks. What is it?"
- "If a friend kept stalling on this, what would you guess was going on?"
- "Is this yours, or was it handed to you?"

### Passed hard deadline

No red, no scolding, straight to options:

> **The date for this passed yesterday.** Some deadlines bend. Want help with a quick 'running late' message, a new date, or letting it go?

### Auto-decay (stale tasks)

Silent. No notification. A small line in the Resting shelf:

> "These drifted out of view. They're here if you want them."

Optional aphorism on that shelf: *"There could be no happiness, no cheerfulness, no hope, no pride, no present, without forgetfulness."* (*Genealogy of Morals* II §1)

### Notifications (scarce: default max 6/day)

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Implementation intention fires | "Coffee's done? Open the report and write one bullet." |
| Time remaining | "About one episode left before you need to leave." |
| Transition warning | "Wrapping-up time: three songs left. Park a note for where you are." |
| Hyperfocus check (overrun) | "You've been in this a while. Water? Body? Then carry on if you like." |
| Gentle resurfacing | "The dentist call is still here. First step: open their contact." |

Never: "Don't forget…", "You haven't…", "Reminder: overdue". Rotate wording; repeated identical alerts habituate fast.

### Overcoming evidence (Selbstüberwindung)

Only when the data is real (minimum sample sizes in the generator). One fact, one comparison, no exclamation mark.

- "In July it took you about 40 minutes to start writing. This month: about 6. Look at you now."
- "You held deep focus for two episodes on Tuesday. In August, one was the limit."
- "Your time guesses are 30% closer to reality than three months ago."
- "Slipped tasks got restarted twice as fast this month."

### Energy check-in (one tap, optional)

> "How much force have you got right now?"
> `plenty` · `some` · `not much` · `fried`

On **fried**: "Then today is autopilot day. Here are three things that need almost nothing from you." *(no guilt, no "push through")*

### The day's allocation (will to power as energy)

> **Today's force:** about two episodes deep, a few songs of medium, plenty of autopilot.
> **Spent on:** the chapter (feeds *a writer*), the landlord email, laundry.
> *30% left unallocated. On purpose.*

### Empty states

- Empty list: "Nothing here. Either you're done, or you're about to capture something. Both are good."
- Empty Becoming: "Who are you becoming? You don't have to know yet."
- Empty Released shelf: "Nothing released yet. Letting go is a skill too."

### Error states

Errors are the app's fault, so the app apologizes. The user never does.

- "That didn't save. Our fault. It's stored on your phone and will sync when it can."
- "Couldn't generate a first step just now. Here's a simple one: open the thing."

## Aphorism library (curated, ship-safe)

Every line must be a **real** Nietzsche passage, with a source. Translations vary, so verify the wording against Kaufmann or Hollingdale before shipping. Each one is tagged with where it may appear.

| Line (paraphrase-faithful) | Source | Use on |
|---|---|---|
| "You shall become the person you are." | *Gay Science* §270 | Onboarding, Becoming tab |
| "We want to become those we are: human beings who are new, unique, incomparable, who give themselves laws, who create themselves." | *Gay Science* §335 | Becoming tab |
| "If we have our own why of life, we shall get along with almost any how." | *Twilight*, Maxims §12 | Identity-link card |
| "One must still have chaos in oneself to be able to give birth to a dancing star." | *Zarathustra*, Prologue §5 | Amor fati onboarding, Capture screen |
| "My formula for greatness in a human being is *amor fati*: that one wants nothing to be different…" | *Ecce Homo*, "Why I Am So Clever" §10 | After a "toll" choice |
| "Looking away shall be my only negation." | *Gay Science* §276 | Released shelf |
| "What is good is light; whatever is divine moves on tender feet." | *Case of Wagner* §1 | Completion (rare) |
| "Sit as little as possible; give no credence to any thought that was not born outdoors while one moved about freely." | *Ecce Homo*, "Why I Am So Clever" §1 | Movement primer before deep work |
| "To 'give style' to one's character: a great and rare art!" | *Gay Science* §290 | Reshape-routine screen |
| "There could be no happiness… no present, without forgetfulness." | *Genealogy* II §1 | Resting/decay shelf |

**Never use misattributed quotes.** Common fakes: "And those who were seen dancing were thought to be insane by those who could not hear the music" (not Nietzsche); "The individual has always had to struggle to keep from being overwhelmed by the tribe" (not Nietzsche).

**Never use:** anything about the Übermensch as a superiority claim, anything about pity or weakness aimed at the user, anything that romanticizes suffering.

## Visual tone (the brief, in words)

- **Dark, confident, minimal, text-forward.** Near-black background (not pure #000), off-white ink, a single warm accent (amber or gold: Sils-Maria at dusk, not a neon dashboard).
- Generous whitespace. One thing per screen where possible.
- Typography carries the personality: a serif with character for headlines and first steps, a clean sans for metadata.
- The "win" color (completion) is the only saturated moment in the UI, so it actually lands.
- Themes rotate weekly or monthly (Sils-Maria, Turin in January, High Noon, Genoa harbor) for novelty. They change palette and completion animation, not the layout. Familiar structure, fresh surface.

## The litmus test

Before shipping any string, ask:

1. Would a kind friend who respects me say this?
2. Does it add a decision, a feeling of debt, or a "should"? → rewrite.
3. Does it tell me what to *do* next in under ~10 words? (for mechanical copy)
4. If it's philosophical: is it the only philosophy on this screen, and can I skip it?
